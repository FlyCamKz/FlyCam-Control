# Branding assets

## FlyCam

`res/Images/flycam_logo.png` was extracted without redrawing from the Word 97-2003
file supplied by the user (`FlyCam Logo .doc`). The source image is a transparent
438 x 187 PNG.

## Satbayev University

`res/Images/satbayev_logo.png` was prepared from the Satbayev University logo
published on Wikimedia Commons, where the source is attributed to Satbayev
University and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Satbayev_University.jpg
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The university's official symbols page was used to verify the identity of the
mark:

- https://official.satbayev.university/en/university/simvolika-universiteta

The background was removed and whitespace was cropped. The mark itself was not
redrawn or altered.
